# arkeun Design & UX System
- **Color Palette:** Warm paper background `#FAF8F5`, elevated card surface `#F5F2EB`, soft warm border `#ECE7DF`, primary ink `#2C2825`, secondary ink `#6E675F`, accents in terracotta `#C86D51`, warm amber `#D99B52`, and sage green `#6B8E78`.
- **Typography & Copy Tone:** Warm, affectionate Korean conversational tone (~해요, ~볼까요?, ~어때요?, ~남겨둘까요?). Strictly eliminate robotic system text (~하십시오, ~선택하세요, ~입력하시오).
- **Mobile Ergonomics (360x800):** Every interactive button, tab, and chip must have a minimum touch target of `44x44px` with generous spacing to prevent mis-taps.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Multi-round or multi-step forms collapse completed rounds into clean 1-line summary pills while keeping only the active round expanded.
